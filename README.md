# Monster Flavor Explorer

Build a premium responsive website called MONSTER FLAVOR LAB — an independent visual catalog of real, verified Monster Energy flavors from India, Asia, and international markets. Dark futuristic aesthetic: black/charcoal, neon green accents, glassmorphism, bold condensed typography, premium product cards, subtle glow, smooth gradients and micro-interactions. Hero: “MONSTER FLAVOR LAB” and “Explore the flavors. Discover the regions.” Navigation: EXPLORE | INDIA | ASIA | IMPORTED | FLAVORS | ABOUT. Create searchable/filterable catalog with ALL | INDIA | ASIA | INTERNATIONAL | ULTRA | JUICE | ORIGINAL. Product cards show name, flavor, region, category, verified info and VIEW FLAVOR. Use only real products verified through official Monster regional websites; do not invent products or claim imported products are available in India without verification. Include verified examples such as Monster Energy Original Green, Monster Ultra/Zero Ultra, Monster JUICE Pipeline Punch, Ultra Paradise, Ultra Peachy Keen, Ultra Violet, Ultra Vice Guava, Fantasy Ruby Red, Mango Loco, Bad Apple, Ripper and Pipeline Punch, while clearly labeling region and verification status. Selecting a flavor should smoothly change background/glow to match the flavor, scale the product, show subtle liquid/fruit effects, and open a premium detail modal with NAME | REGION | FLAVOR | CATEGORY | VERIFIED INFO | OFFICIAL SOURCE. Add MY FLAVOR LIST favorites using local persistence. About text: “An independent visual catalog exploring Monster Energy products and regional flavor variations. Product availability varies by country.” and “Not affiliated with or endorsed by Monster Energy.” No shopping, purchasing, payments, checkout, ordering or delivery. Footer with official Monster regional website links and trademark/image ownership notice. Make it feel like a premium interactive flavor museum, not an online store. Prioritize polished UX, accessibility, fast loading, responsive mobile/desktop layouts, and restrained animation.
